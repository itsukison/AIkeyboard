# Organic content system — 敬語ボタン

Last updated: **2026-07-20**. Owner: Itsuki.

This is the working backlog for TikTok Photo Mode/video, Instagram Reels/carousels,
X, and later RED. Scores are hypotheses to test, not forecasts.

## Decision

The account should not become another generic 「正しい敬語」 account. The ownable
position is:

> **送りづらい仕事のメッセージを、その場で送れる文章にするキーボード。**

The recurring story is **awkward real-life message → tension/comedy → actual
in-keyboard transformation → sendable result**. This both entertains and teaches
the exact job the product performs.

Until activated-user W1 retention reaches 25%, this is a small organic learning
program, not an acquisition scale-up. Do not buy reach, seed creators, or optimize
for downloads alone. Content should help us learn which situations attract users
who run and accept a rewrite.

## Audiences

1. **Primary:** Japanese job hunters, new graduates, and early-career office
   workers who freeze before messaging a boss, recruiter, senior, or client.
2. **Adjacent high-intent:** Any worker who knows what they mean but cannot make
   the message sound appropriately polite.
3. **Secondary/localized:** Chinese speakers living or working in Japan. The pain
   is real, but RED/`zh` traffic currently retains about half as well as Japanese
   organic traffic. Localize proven business scenarios later; do not let this
   redefine the core product promise.
4. **Reach-only experiment:** people watching relationship/crush chats. They are
   not the ICP unless the story is honestly about rewriting tone, not dating
   advice.

## Score key

- **Effort:** 1 = under ~45 minutes after a template exists; 5 = filming,
  multiple people, or several hours of editing.
- **Reach:** expected ability to earn views/shares, 1–5.
- **Qualified impact:** likelihood of attracting or activating the actual
  business-message user, 1–5. This matters more than reach.
- **Retention value:** ability to teach a feature or repeatable use case that
  could make an existing user return, 1–5.

## Assessment of the first three ideas

The sharper fictional workplace-conflict direction developed from the boss-chat
idea is maintained in `spicy-content-bank.md`. It contains the 「丁寧にキレる」
positioning, creative rationale, episode flow, scores, scripts, and editorial
guardrails.

### 1. Easy keigo phrase slideshows

Keep them, but make them a **supporting format rather than the identity of the
account**. They are cheap and saveable, but the collected references show how
interchangeable the category looks: grids of phrases, red × / red ○, textbook
paper, and generic office imagery.

The product bridge should be:

1. specific situation, not “敬語10選”;
2. one blunt draft viewers recognize;
3. one sendable full-message rewrite;
4. a real keyboard capture saying 「暗記できなくても、送る前に整えられる」.

Best initial episodes:

- 「確認してください」を上司に催促するとき
- 体調不良で休む朝の一文
- 締切に間に合わないと分かった瞬間
- 面接日程を変えてもらいたいとき
- 返信が来ない相手への二度目の連絡

### 2. Funny boss chats

This is the strongest idea. It has entertainment, product fit, seriality, and a
natural comment prompt. The existing
`content/001-boss-progress/` template already establishes the right safe pattern:
a fictional generic chat, a joke, a blunt draft, a sendable rewrite, and a light
product reveal.

The improvement to test is **showing the real keyboard action one slide earlier**.
The current first concept waits until slide 6 for the product. One variant should
insert a real tap/replace capture at slide 4 or 5, while the control keeps the
late reveal.

Episode bank:

- 「今日中にいける？」を移動距離だと思った新卒
- 37.3度で休んでいいか上司に確認し続ける新人
- 「なるはやで」を今日中だと思わなかった新人
- 上司の「一旦持ち帰ります」を荷物の話だと思った新人
- CCに部長を入れ忘れてからの謝罪
- 金曜17:58の「月曜朝イチで」
- 既読を付けたまま返信を忘れた翌朝
- 誤字を指摘されたのに試合結果だと思った新人

Text-only episodes should take roughly 45–90 minutes after the existing template
is generalized. A filmed version is optional and likely 2–4 hours; it should be
tested only after the slideshow proves the script.

### 3. Crush chats

Treat this as a **reach experiment, not a pillar**. The viral mechanic is useful,
but “this app helps you win your crush” promises a product that does not exist.
The honest version is limited to tone:

- 「好きな人への返信、重すぎる文章を自然にした」
- 「初デート後のお礼、長文すぎるので短くした」
- 「先輩に送りたいけど、敬語だと距離が遠すぎる」

Use the `自然に` or a clearly shown custom prompt, never imply romantic strategy,
compatibility prediction, or conversation coaching. Judge it on qualified profile
visits and downstream rewrite acceptance, not views. If those are weak, stop after
one test.

## Reusable creative system

### Chat-story carousel (6–7 slides)

1. **Hook:** one frightening/funny incoming message in very large text.
2. **Escalation:** the bad first response or misunderstanding.
3. **Consequence:** boss/client reaction.
4. **Viewer question:** 「このあと何て返す？」
5. **Product proof:** actual keyboard capture, tap, candidate, replace.
6. **Sendable result:** highlight apology, request, alternative date, etc.
7. **CTA:** 「返しづらかったメッセージをコメントで教えて」.

### Fast proof video (10–20 seconds)

1. First frame: the bad draft, not the logo.
2. Screen recording: tap the relevant button within the first few seconds.
3. Show at least two candidates briefly, then replace.
4. End on the sent-ready message and one narrow CTA: save, comment, or try it.

### Educational carousel (4–6 slides)

1. Situation-specific hook.
2. Common but risky wording.
3. Better wording plus one-line explanation.
4. A second context or caveat.
5. Real keyboard proof: “you do not have to memorize it.”
6. Save CTA plus a request for the next situation.

TikTok's current official guidance reinforces a hook/body/close structure,
native vertical creative, text overlays, sound, an early product connection, and
testing multiple hooks. Use those as production principles, not as guarantees of
performance.

## Four-week pilot — eight posts

This is deliberately small while retention remains below the Phase 0 gate.

| Post | Series | Working hook | What it tests |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | 上司チャット救命室 | Existing 「今日中にいける？」 carousel | Baseline: story shares/comments and late product reveal |
| 2 | 3秒で送れる文に | 「本日中は無理です。明日やります。」 | Pure product proof and accepted-rewrite intent |
| 3 | この返信、どうする？ | 「進捗どう？」にまだ終わっていない時 | Reply-feature discovery |
| 4 | 仕事の地雷文レスキュー | 休む朝、上司に送る一文 | Saves from a high-frequency use case |
| 5 | 上司チャット救命室 | 37.3度なのに休んでよいか聞き続ける新人 | Repeated comedy format and early product reveal |
| 6 | 同じ本音、相手別 | 「少し待って」を同僚・上司・取引先へ | Context education versus generic phrase content |
| 7 | コメントを敬語にします | Best real question from posts 1–6 | Community flywheel and genuine pain discovery |
| 8 | 好きな人への返信 | One honest `自然に` rewrite | Whether broad reach has any qualified downstream value |

Publish roughly twice per week. Cross-post the same core asset, but adapt the
caption and crop instead of rebuilding it. No paid boost during the pilot.

## What to measure

Views are diagnostic, not the win condition. Record per post:

| Layer | Metrics | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 3-second hold / first-slide continuation | Did the situation stop the scroll? |
| Value | Completion, saves, shares | Was it useful or socially recognizable? |
| Community | Comments that contain an actual message/situation | Did it uncover real jobs-to-be-done? |
| Intent | Profile visits, App Store link taps | Did viewers want the product rather than only the joke? |
| Product | First rewrite run and first candidate accepted by source/cohort | Did the content set up a successful first experience? |
| Quality | Activated W1 retention by source/cohort | Did it attract users who keep the keyboard? |

The current onboarding source survey puts both App Store and RED into `other`, so
series-level attribution is not yet trustworthy. Before calling a format a winner,
use distinct trackable links/campaign identifiers where the platform permits and
add explicit RED/App Store/content-source options to the measurement plan. Until
then, annotate posting dates and treat install spikes only as directional.

## Editorial guardrails

- Show only capabilities that ship now: rewrite, candidates, replace, reply via
  copy-and-tap, email/natural/English prompts, refinements, and custom prompts.
- Never claim dating expertise, automatic access to a conversation, or a shipped
  Chinese keyboard mode.
- Normal typing is local; text goes to Cloud AI only after an explicit AI tap.
  This can become a trust post, but do not imply Cloud AI itself is on-device.
- Label invented chats as fiction and never expose real names, employers, or
  private messages without permission.
- Say 「この場面なら送れる例」 rather than presenting context-dependent
  business Japanese as the only universally correct answer.
- Quality-check every published rewrite. Current data says 58% of users who run
  AI have never accepted a candidate; content must not hide weak outputs or train
  expectations the product cannot meet.
- Use one CTA per post. Prefer “what message should we rescue next?” because it
  generates both engagement and product research.
